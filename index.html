Пик Стрима

The peak of the stream

Пик Стрима (Pik Strima) is a clips channel: short vertical videos of the best moments from live streams, each one cut around the moment the chat reacted to most.

The clips are made by the channel's own tool, which runs on the channel owner's computer. It watches public live streams, finds the moments, and prepares each clip with captions. The owner reviews every clip and posts it to the channel's accounts.

Where to watch

YouTube: @pikstrima

Streamers and rights holders

If a clip shows your stream and you want it changed or removed, say so through the channel's page and it will be taken down.
