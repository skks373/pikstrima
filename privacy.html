Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. This website

This website is a set of plain pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. The company that hosts it may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policy.

2. The Pik Strima app

The app runs on the channel owner's own computer and is used by the owner only. When the owner connects one of the channel's accounts (TikTok, YouTube or Instagram), the platform gives the app:

  • an access token for that account,
  • the account's identifier and display name.

The token is stored encrypted on that computer. It is used for one thing: sending the channel's clips to that account. It is not shared with anyone and not sold.

3. Viewers and streamers

The app collects no data about the people who watch the clips. To find the moments worth clipping it measures how active a stream's public chat is; it does not publish chat messages or usernames.

4. Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the platform's own settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions). After that the stored token no longer works, and it is deleted on request.

5. Changes and contact

This policy may change; the date above shows the latest version. Questions and requests go through the channel's page: youtube.com/@pikstrima.
